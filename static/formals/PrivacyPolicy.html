Your information, your privacy

iPrepare is a platform for quiz practice, performance tracking, and interactive challenges. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and explain our data practices clearly. The information we collect depends on the features you use and the services enabled in your account.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how iPrepare ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and discloses information when you access or use our website, application, and related services.

By using iPrepare, you acknowledge this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will obtain that consent separately.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Service. If you do not agree with our data practices, please discontinue use of the platform and contact us with any privacy concerns.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account information

When you register or use an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Name or display name.
  • Email address and account identifier.
  • Authentication information needed to verify your identity and maintain your account.
  • Profile information that you choose to provide.

We do not intend to collect your account password in readable form. Where passwords are managed by our application, they should be stored using an appropriate secure password-hashing method.

2.2 Quiz and performance information

To provide quizzes and progress-tracking features, we may process:

  • Quiz attempts, selected answers, and scores.
  • Topics, subjects, and exam categories selected.
  • Number of questions attempted and correct answers.
  • Quiz dates, durations, completion status, and related performance statistics, where recorded.
  • Bookmarks and saved questions.
  • Challenge participation, results, and rankings, where these features are available.

2.3 Technical and usage information

When you use iPrepare, our servers or enabled third-party services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request timestamps, diagnostic logs, and information about interactions with the platform.

The exact information collected depends on our hosting, security, analytics, and application configuration.

2.4 Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact support, report a problem, or send feedback, we may collect the information you include in your message and any information reasonably necessary to respond.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information for the following purposes, where applicable and permitted by law:

  • Create and manage user accounts and authenticate users.
  • Deliver quizzes, calculate scores, and display results.
  • Track quiz history, bookmarks, and study progress.
  • Support challenge mode and display relevant competition results.
  • Maintain the security, stability, and functionality of the application.
  • Diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and investigate suspected fraud or misuse.
  • Respond to questions, feedback, and support requests.
  • Meet legal obligations and enforce our terms where necessary.

We will not use personal information for a materially different purpose without an appropriate legal basis or any additional consent required by law.

4. Quizzes, Progress, and Challenges

iPrepare processes quiz and activity information to calculate scores and provide progress statistics. This information may be associated with your account so that you can review your previous activity.

If you participate in challenge mode, certain information, such as your display name, score, or challenge outcome, may be visible to the other participant or to other users if the relevant feature displays public results or rankings.

We recommend avoiding sensitive personal information in your display name or any optional profile field. We do not promise that challenge participation or account activity will be anonymous to other participants.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information as part of the ordinary operation described by this policy. We may disclose information in the following circumstances, when applicable:

  • Service providers: To hosting providers, database providers, email services, security providers, and other vendors that help operate iPrepare.
  • Other users: To the extent necessary for challenge mode, leaderboards, or other features that display participant information.
  • Legal requirements: When disclosure is required by law, a valid legal process, or a competent authority.
  • Security and protection: When reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect the platform, or safeguard users' rights and safety, subject to applicable law.
  • Business changes: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable legal requirements and appropriate safeguards.

Where third parties process personal information on our behalf, we aim to use appropriate contractual and security safeguards.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

iPrepare or its service providers may use cookies, local storage, session identifiers, or similar technologies to support functions such as maintaining login sessions, protecting accounts, remembering preferences, and improving reliability.

Whether analytics or other non-essential technologies are used depends on the services enabled on our platform. Where consent is required, we will seek it before using the relevant technologies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential storage or cookies may affect login sessions or other features.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Depending on our implementation, safeguards may include access controls, secure authentication, encryption in transit, protected database access, backups, and security monitoring.

No website, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps and provide notifications where required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including providing the service, maintaining account history, resolving disputes, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information, its purpose, applicable law, and technical requirements.

You may request account deletion or deletion of eligible personal information by contacting us through the details below or using an available account-deletion feature.

Following a valid request, we will handle deletion in accordance with applicable law and our retention obligations. Some information may need to be retained for a lawful reason, and residual copies may remain temporarily in protected backups until those backups are rotated or deleted.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request information about personal data we process.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, subject to legal and practical limitations.
  • Raise a grievance or submit a privacy-related complaint through the appropriate channel.
  • Exercise any additional rights provided by applicable data-protection law.

To exercise a right, contact us using the details in the Contact Us section. We may need to verify your identity before responding. We will handle requests within the time limits prescribed by applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

iPrepare may be used by students, including minors. Where users are children under applicable law, we will handle their personal information in accordance with the requirements that apply to the service and the relevant age group.

Where required by law, we will obtain verifiable parental or guardian consent before processing a child's personal data and implement applicable protections.

We do not intend to collect more personal information from children than is reasonably necessary for the relevant service. If you believe a child's information has been collected or processed improperly, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services and Links

iPrepare may rely on external providers for services such as hosting, databases, authentication, email delivery, security, or analytics. If enabled, these providers may process information as necessary to deliver their services.

The platform may also link to third-party websites. We do not control the privacy practices of websites that we do not operate. Please review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

We will update this policy or provide additional notices where required to explain material data processing arrangements.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our services, data practices, or legal requirements. When material changes are made, we will take reasonable steps to notify users where appropriate or required by law.

The latest version will display its effective or last-updated date. Where required, we will obtain renewed consent before implementing changes that require it.
